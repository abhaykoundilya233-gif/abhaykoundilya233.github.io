ABOUT THE PROJECT

A place for evidence.

Āraṇyānāṃ Pataye — Archaeology of Āryāvarta.

Āraṇyānāṃ Pataye is an independent digital research archive dedicated to exploring the historical, archaeological, epigraphic, textual and artistic record of the Indian subcontinent.

The archive is intended as a place to collect observations, examine sources, discuss historical questions and make research easier to explore. Its subjects include inscriptions, manuscripts, coins, architecture, archaeology and art history.

THE ARCHIVE'S APPROACH

Our approach

01

Source-led inquiry

Give attention to the primary source, its provenance, language, date and material context.

02

Clear distinctions

Where possible, distinguish what a source states from an interpretation or a hypothesis.

03

Research in progress

Historical study evolves. Corrections and new evidence should be acknowledged as part of the process.

Questions or research enquiries?
